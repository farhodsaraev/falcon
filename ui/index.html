Connections

AI key

Add another key or replace the current one. OpenAI, OpenRouter and Anthropic keys are recognised by their shape. An OpenAI key is also what gives Falcon his voice. Keys stay on this computer.

Telegram

Talk to Falcon from your phone.

  1. Open Telegram on your phone or computer.
  2. In the search box type BotFather and open the one with the blue tick.
  3. Press Start, then send the message /newbot
  4. It asks for a name. Type anything, for example Falcon.
  5. It asks for a username. It must be unique and end in bot, for example anna_falcon_bot. If it is taken, try another.
  6. BotFather replies with a long line that looks like 123456789:AAE…. That is the token. Copy it and keep it private.
  7. Paste the token here and press Connect.
  1. Back in Telegram, search for the username you just chose and open your new bot.
  2. Press Start and send it any message, for example hello.
  3. The bot replies with a short code. Type that code here and press Confirm. From then on, Falcon answers you in that chat.

Morning briefing

Once a day Falcon sends you the weather, today's meetings and important mail in Telegram, without being asked. Connect Telegram first.

Your own Chrome

Lets Falcon work in sites you are already signed in to, such as your mail and calendar. Chrome asks you to allow this yourself.

  1. In Chrome, open chrome://inspect/#remote-debugging
  2. Switch on Allow remote debugging.
  3. Press Check below, then click Allow when Chrome asks.
