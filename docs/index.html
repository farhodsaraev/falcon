Install Falcon

Falcon installs on a computer, not a phone. Open this page on your Mac or Windows PC and follow the steps there.

Have one thing ready: an AI key. It is a long line of letters that starts with sk-. The simplest is an OpenAI key, which gives Falcon both his mind and his voice: create one at platform.openai.com/api-keys. Anthropic and OpenRouter keys also work.

  1. Open Terminal

    Hold ⌘ Command and press Space. Type Terminal. Press Return.

    a small window with a line of text and a blinking cursor.

  2. Copy this line

    Press the Copy button. Do not type the line by hand.

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/farhodsaraev/falcon/main/installer/install.sh | bash

  3. Paste it into Terminal

    Click inside the Terminal window. Hold ⌘ Command and press V. Then press Return.

  4. Wait a few minutes

    Leave the window open. Nothing will ask you for a password. When it says Done., Falcon's window opens by itself.

  5. Give Falcon your AI key

    In Falcon's window: paste your AI key, tick the box, press Start. The first start takes a few minutes; wait until he greets you.

  6. Say hello

    Type a request, or press the microphone and simply talk.

Opening Falcon later: double-click Falcon on your Desktop or in Applications.

macOS asks for permission the first time Falcon looks at your screen or controls an app. Click Allow.

If it says “Falcon could not be installed”: check your internet connection and repeat steps 2 and 3. Running it twice is safe.

  1. Open PowerShell

    Press the ⊞ Windows key. Type PowerShell. Press Enter. Do not choose “Run as administrator”; it is not needed.

    a dark window with a line of text and a blinking cursor.

  2. Copy this line

    Press the Copy button. Do not type the line by hand.

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/farhodsaraev/falcon/main/installer/install.ps1 | iex

  3. Paste it into PowerShell

    Click inside the PowerShell window. Hold Ctrl and press V. Then press Enter.

  4. Wait a few minutes

    Leave the window open. Nothing will ask you for a password. When it says Done., Falcon's window opens by itself.
